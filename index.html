SLA Breach Auditor Privacy Policy

Effective date: 15 October 2026

This policy describes how the independent developer publishing as Fangniude handles information through the SLA Breach Auditor app for Jira Service Management Cloud (the “App”).

Information the App processes

The App reads Jira issues and native Jira Service Management SLA data through Atlassian APIs, using the permissions of the Jira user who starts or views a report. To create, save, display, rerun, and export reports, the App processes and may store:

  • Atlassian account IDs and display names for report creators, assignees at an SLA breach, current assignees, and relevant assignee changes;
  • Jira issue IDs and keys, project or JQL report filters, selected SLA metrics, SLA cycle and breach timestamps, report time zone, and report configuration;
  • limited evidence and status information needed to explain report results, including relevant assignee history and data-quality warnings.

The App does not retain complete Jira changelog payloads. CSV exports are generated for the requesting user and are not kept as a separate server-side export by the App.

Purposes and access

The App uses this information only to calculate SLA breach reports, preserve report runs and their supporting evidence, enforce the creator’s access to saved reports, and maintain accurate Atlassian account information. Report access is checked against the creator’s current Jira visibility. Installing the App does not give a user broader access to Jira issues.

The App periodically reports Atlassian account IDs that remain in its stored records to Atlassian’s privacy-maintenance API. Atlassian can notify the App when an account has been closed or its profile has changed; the App then erases or refreshes the associated stored identity information.

Storage, location, and service providers

App records are stored in Atlassian Forge hosted storage, scoped to the relevant Atlassian app installation. The App does not send report data to a developer-operated external server, advertising service, or analytics service. Atlassian provides the Forge runtime, storage, and Jira APIs and processes data as described in its Forge terms and Privacy Policy.

Forge hosted storage follows the data residency location configured for the customer’s Atlassian app. The actual location depends on that configuration and Atlassian’s current Forge data residency behavior. See Atlassian’s Forge data residency documentation for current details.

This policy is hosted as a public page by GitHub Pages. GitHub may separately process technical information about visits to this policy page, such as IP address, device or browser information, and request time, under the GitHub General Privacy Statement. This page hosting does not give GitHub access to the App’s Forge storage.

Retention and deletion

  • Saved report definitions remain until the user deletes them.
  • Report runs and results are retained for no more than 12 months from creation. Diagnostic details for failed runs are retained for no more than 30 days.
  • Deleting a saved report makes it unavailable immediately; removal of its stored records and related evidence is completed through background cleanup.
  • After the App is uninstalled, Atlassian Forge hosted storage retains its data for up to 28 days before deletion under Atlassian’s storage lifecycle.

Your choices and privacy requests

You can delete saved reports in the App and stop further processing by uninstalling it. To request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information processed by the App, contact the publisher using the customer support contact shown on the SLA Breach Auditor Marketplace listing or the Fangniude Atlassian Marketplace partner profile. Atlassian account and Jira data may also be subject to the controls and privacy processes of your Atlassian organization.

Security and changes

Atlassian states that Forge hosted storage is encrypted in transit and at rest and isolated per app installation. The publisher uses the App’s access controls and Atlassian’s hosted security features to protect stored records. This policy will be updated if the App’s data practices materially change; the effective date above will be revised.

《SLA Breach Auditor》隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 15 日

本政策说明以 Fangniude 名义发布应用的个人开发者，如何处理 Jira Service Management Cloud 应用《SLA Breach Auditor》（“本应用”）中的信息。

本应用处理的信息

本应用通过 Atlassian API 读取 Jira 工单和 Jira Service Management 原生 SLA 数据，并使用发起或查看报告的 Jira 用户自身权限。为计算、保存、展示、重新运行和导出报告，本应用会处理并可能保存：

  • 报告创建者、SLA 违约时的经办人、当前经办人及相关经办人变更记录中的 Atlassian 账户 ID 和显示名称；
  • Jira 工单 ID 和键、项目或 JQL 报告筛选条件、所选 SLA 指标、SLA 周期和违约时间、报告时区及报告配置；
  • 解释报告结果所需的有限证据和状态信息，包括相关经办人历史及数据质量提示。

本应用不保存完整的 Jira 变更历史原始数据。CSV 导出由本应用为提出请求的用户生成，不会作为独立的服务器端导出文件留存。

处理目的和访问控制

本应用仅将这些信息用于计算 SLA 违约报告、保存报告运行结果及其必要证据、控制创建者对已保存报告的访问，以及维护准确的 Atlassian 账户信息。查看报告时会依据创建者当前的 Jira 可见权限进行检查。安装本应用不会扩大用户对 Jira 工单的访问权限。

本应用会定期向 Atlassian 的隐私维护 API 报告仍保存在记录中的 Atlassian 账户 ID。若 Atlassian 通知账户已关闭或个人资料已变更，本应用会相应删除或更新关联的身份信息。

存储、位置和服务提供方

应用记录保存在 Atlassian Forge 托管存储中，并按相关 Atlassian 应用安装进行隔离。本应用不会将报告数据发送到开发者运营的外部服务器、广告服务或分析服务。Atlassian 提供 Forge 运行环境、存储和 Jira API，并依据其 Forge 条款及隐私政策处理数据。

Forge 托管存储遵循客户为 Atlassian 应用配置的数据驻留位置。实际位置取决于该配置以及 Atlassian 当前的 Forge 数据驻留规则。详情请参阅 Atlassian 的 Forge 数据驻留说明。

本政策页面由 GitHub Pages 公开托管。GitHub 可能根据其《GitHub 通用隐私声明》，另行处理访问本政策页面时的技术信息，例如 IP 地址、设备或浏览器信息及请求时间。托管本页面不会使 GitHub 获得本应用 Forge 存储中的数据。

保存期限和删除

  • 已保存的报告定义会保留至用户将其删除。
  • 报告运行结果最多保存至创建后 12 个月；失败运行的诊断信息最多保存 30 天。
  • 删除已保存报告后，该报告会立即不可见；其存储记录和相关证据随后通过后台清理完成删除。
  • 卸载本应用后，Atlassian Forge 托管存储会按 Atlassian 的存储生命周期最多保留相关数据 28 天，然后删除。

你的选择和隐私请求

你可以在本应用中删除已保存的报告，也可以卸载本应用以停止后续处理。如需查询、更正或删除本应用处理的个人信息，请通过《SLA Breach Auditor》Marketplace listing 或 Fangniude Atlassian Marketplace 开发者资料页所示的客户支持联系方式联系开发者。Atlassian 账户和 Jira 数据也可能受你所在 Atlassian 组织的管理控制和隐私流程约束。

安全和政策变更

Atlassian 说明 Forge 托管存储在传输和静态存储时均加密，并按应用安装隔离。开发者通过本应用的访问控制及 Atlassian 托管安全功能保护存储记录。如果本应用的数据处理方式发生重大变化，将更新本政策及上方生效日期。
