SLA Breach Auditor — User Guide

For Jira Service Management Cloud

SLA Breach Auditor reviews historical breaches of native Jira Service Management SLA metrics. It reconstructs the Jira assignee at the recorded breach time from available Jira history and shows when the evidence is missing or ambiguous.

Run a report

  1. Open SLA Breach Auditor from the Jira app navigation.
  2. Choose a project scope or enter an advanced JQL scope.
  3. Select the native SLA metrics, date range, and report time zone.
  4. Start the report run and review its results when it completes.

Review and reuse results

Review breach records, attribution states, evidence traces, coverage, and warnings. Use the available filters and aggregations to narrow the results. You can save a report definition for later and manually rerun it; a rerun creates a new report run using the inputs resolved at that time. Export the currently filtered results to CSV.

Licensing and retained data

  • Trial or active subscription: create Saved Reports and Report Runs, and manually Rerun a Saved Report.
  • Inactive, missing, or unverifiable subscription: new reports and Reruns are blocked. Previously retained reports remain available to their authorized creators for permitted reading and CSV export. Deletion and privacy maintenance remain available.
  • An already verified in-progress Report Run may finish or undergo a bounded Retry under its recorded Entitlement Snapshot, subject to current Jira visibility, retention, and deletion rules. This does not authorize a new user-initiated Rerun.

The app audits historical SLA data. It does not provide live alerts, scheduled reports, automatic SLA corrections, prediction, or performance scoring. When historical evidence is incomplete or ambiguous, the result shows the relevant uncertainty instead of guessing.

Access and limitations

Reports are private to their creator. Jira visibility is checked using the creator’s current permissions when records are read. Missing, contradictory, permission-limited, or ambiguous history is reported as a limitation rather than guessed. The app does not change Jira issues or SLA settings.

Results are audit evidence and are not a verdict about an individual’s performance or responsibility.

Privacy

Read the SLA Breach Auditor Privacy Policy for information about data processing, storage, retention, deletion, and privacy requests.

《SLA Breach Auditor》使用指南

适用于 Jira Service Management Cloud

《SLA Breach Auditor》用于复核 Jira Service Management 原生 SLA 指标的历史违约记录。应用会根据可用的 Jira 历史记录还原违约时的工单经办人，并明确显示证据缺失或含糊的情况。

运行报告

  1. 从 Jira 应用导航中打开 SLA Breach Auditor。
  2. 选择项目范围，或输入高级 JQL 范围。
  3. 选择原生 SLA 指标、日期范围和报告时区。
  4. 启动报告运行，并在完成后查看结果。

复核和复用结果

查看违约记录、归因状态、证据轨迹、覆盖情况和警告。使用可用的筛选和汇总缩小结果范围。你可以保存报告定义以便稍后手动重新运行；重新运行会根据当时解析的输入创建一份新的报告运行结果。当前筛选结果可导出为 CSV。

授权和已保存数据

  • Trial 或有效订阅：可以创建 Saved Report、执行 Report Run，并手动 Rerun 已保存报告。
  • 订阅失效、缺失或无法核验：禁止创建新报告和发起 Rerun。此前保留的报告仍可由有权限的创建者在当前 Jira 授权范围内查看和导出 CSV；删除和隐私维护仍可用。
  • 已经核验授权且正在执行的 Report Run，可以依据其记录的 Entitlement Snapshot 完成或进行有限 Retry，但仍受当前 Jira 可见性、保留期和删除规则约束。这不等于允许用户新发起 Rerun。

本应用审计历史 SLA 数据，不提供实时提醒、定时报表、自动 SLA 修复、预测或绩效评分。历史证据不完整或含糊时，结果会显示相应不确定性，不会猜测。

访问控制和功能范围

报告仅对创建者可见。读取记录时，应用会依据创建者当前权限检查 Jira 可见性。对于缺失、矛盾、受权限限制或含糊的历史信息，应用会显示相应限制，不会猜测。本应用不会修改 Jira 工单或 SLA 设置。

报告是审计证据，不用于判定个人绩效或责任。

隐私

数据处理、存储、保留期限、删除和隐私请求方式请参阅《SLA Breach Auditor 隐私政策》。
